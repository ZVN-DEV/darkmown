# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Darkmown to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, background, or identity. Everyone who takes part — contributors, maintainers, and people filing issues — agrees to keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith and be patient with newcomers.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit history — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer via a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/ZVN-DEV/darkmown/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer listed in `package.json`. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may warn or ban anyone for behavior they judge inappropriate, harmful, or unprofessional.

---

This is a deliberately short, project-specific code of conduct. For a fuller standard, see the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
